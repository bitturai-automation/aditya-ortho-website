What types of orthopedic supports do you offer?

Our catalogue covers categories such as knee, back, cervical, ankle and foot, wrist and hand, shoulder, elbow, mobility and other support products.

The catalogue is designed to showcase Samson Orthotics, Tynor, Dynamic Techno Medicals and UM Orthotics products available through Aditya Ortho, subject to local stock and brand authorization.

Yes. Where size information is available in the catalogue, it is shown directly on the product card. You can also enquire with the store.
